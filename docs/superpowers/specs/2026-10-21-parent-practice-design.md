## Intent and novelty
Give parents a reusable short practice mix for one child's existing age mode, with reliable per-question resume and a factual recap across sessions/days. Existing eight islands,30 sequential stages×3 tasks, two age groups, multiple local profiles, packaged DE/EN prompts and simple least-progress next-stage recommendation are preserved. No existing saved mixed-session planner, persistent answer attempts or multi-day practice history was found.

Considered: extend the recommendation text only (too little new workflow); silently choose difficulty from a new mastery score (unsupported educational claim and entitlement risk); chosen explicit parent-curated stage mix with observed practice facts. No diagnostic, developmental assessment, guaranteed learning or independent mastery claim. No child links/sharing/backend/ads/analytics.
